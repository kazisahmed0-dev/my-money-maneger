# 💰 My Money Manager

A modern and user-friendly personal finance management Android app designed to help users track their income, expenses, savings, budgets, and financial goals in one place.

## ✨ Features

- 💰 Track Income
- 💸 Track Expenses
- 💵 Automatically Calculate Balance
- 📅 Daily, Weekly, Monthly & Yearly Reports
- 📊 Income & Expense Analytics
- 📈 Monthly and Yearly Charts
- 🏦 Savings Tracking
- 🎯 Savings Goals
- 💳 Monthly Budget Management
- 🔄 Recurring Income & Expenses
- 🔍 Search & Advanced Filters
- 📝 Custom Income & Expense Names
- 🗂️ Custom Categories
- 📷 Receipt/Image Attachment
- 📤 CSV Data Export
- 💾 Backup & Restore
- 🔐 PIN Lock
- 🌙 Dark & Light Mode
- 🇧🇩 Bangla & English Language Support
- 🔔 Local Notifications
- 📱 Offline Support

## 📊 Yearly Financial Overview

The app automatically organizes financial data by month and year.

For example:

January:
- Income: ৳30,000
- Expense: ৳12,000
- Balance: ৳18,000

February:
- Income: ৳40,000
- Expense: ৳15,000
- Balance: ৳25,000

The yearly dashboard also shows:

- Total yearly income
- Total yearly expense
- Total yearly savings
- Monthly income distribution
- Monthly expense distribution
- Income percentage by category
- Expense percentage by category

## 🎯 Savings Goals

Users can create savings goals such as:

> Buy Laptop

Set a target amount, track saved money, and monitor progress with a visual progress bar.

## 📱 Designed for Android

## 🔒 Privacy

The application is designed with privacy in mind.

Personal financial information should remain private and stored locally unless cloud synchronization is explicitly enabled in a future version.
